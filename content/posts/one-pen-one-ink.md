Last January I put every pen but one in a drawer, filled it with a single bottle
of ordinary blue-black, and left it that way until December.

## What I expected

Boredom, mostly. A constant itch to rotate.

## What actually happened

The itch faded in about three weeks. What replaced it was a kind of fluency — I
stopped thinking about the tool and started thinking about the writing. The nib
wore very slightly to my hand. The ink became invisible in the way a familiar
typeface is invisible.

I have gone back to rotating, but more slowly now, and with far less buying.
