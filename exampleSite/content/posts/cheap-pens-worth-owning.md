A short list I keep coming back to when someone asks where to start without
spending real money.

## The four

- **Pilot Kakuno** — a genuinely good gold-tone-adjacent steel nib in a body
  designed for schoolchildren. The smiley face on the nib grows on you.
- **Platinum Preppy** — the eyedropper-conversion gateway drug. A pack of silicone
  grease and a spare O-ring turns it into a huge-capacity daily writer.
- **Lamy Safari** — the moulded grip is divisive; the nib swaps are not. Buy one
  body, three nibs, and stop thinking about it.
- **Jinhao 82** — an unapologetic Sailor homage for pocket change, with a nib
  smoother than the price suggests.

## The point

None of these will be your endgame pen. All of them will still write in five
years, which is more than some pens ten times the price can claim.
