"Flex" gets used for everything from a nib that gives a little under pressure to a
vintage wet noodle that triples its line width. Here is how I sort them.

## A rough ladder

- **Soft** — springs slightly, adds character, no real line variation.
- **Semi-flex** — noticeable widening with intent; forgiving of technique.
- **Full flex** — wide swells with light pressure; demands a slow hand.
- **Wet noodle** — extreme variation from almost no pressure; unforgiving and rare.

## The honest advice

Start soft. Line variation is a skill before it is a nib, and a springy modern
steel nib will teach you most of what you need to know without the anxiety of
flexing something irreplaceable.
