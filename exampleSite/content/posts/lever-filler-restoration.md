The pen came in a lot of three, none of them writing. This one had the cleanest
barrel and a nib that was only slightly sprung, so it went first.

## The sac

The old sac had turned to black dust inside the barrel. Shellac softened with a
few minutes of gentle heat, the section pulled cleanly, and a fresh size-16 sac
went on with a thin ring of orange shellac and a day to cure.

## The nib

Realigning the tines took three sessions with a loupe and a brass shim, stopping
each time before I lost patience. It writes a wet, slightly stubbish medium now —
not corrected to perfect, but honest, and entirely usable.
