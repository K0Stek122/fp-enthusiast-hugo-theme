The Custom 823 is a vacuum filler, which means the barrel *is* the reservoir — one
pull of the blind cap and it takes on close to two millilitres of ink. For a week
of morning pages that meant filling once and never thinking about it again.

## The party trick

Loosen the blind cap a quarter turn before a long writing session and the ink
feeds noticeably more freely. Tighten it back down for storage or travel and the
seal keeps the nib from burping on a plane.

## After seven days

The medium nib is smooth without being glassy, the amber demonstrator hides
nothing, and the balance posted is better than any pen this size has a right to
be. It is not cheap, but it is the kind of pen you stop noticing — which is the
highest praise I have for a daily writer.
