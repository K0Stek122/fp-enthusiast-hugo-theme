I still have most of a ream of the old 52 gsm Tomoe River, bought in a small panic
when the reformulation was announced. Three years of using both side by side has
made me a lot less precious about it.

## What changed

The new stock is very slightly stiffer and dries a touch faster. Sheen is reduced
but not gone. Shading is essentially identical. Feathering and bleed are still
remarkably well controlled for how thin the sheet is.

## What it means in practice

For a notebook I write in every day, the new paper is arguably the better choice —
pages turn without curling and the ink is dry before my hand reaches it. I am
keeping the hoard for letters, and using the new stuff for everything else.
